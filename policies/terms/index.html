Terms of Service

The small print on commissions, payments and timelines.

Commission agreement

A commission begins when you approve the design + quote and pay a 50% advance. The advance covers materials and the start of handwork; it is non-refundable once production begins.

Timelines

Indicative timelines are given at quoting and confirmed in writing. We prioritise confirmed wedding dates — if your date changes, tell us immediately.

Pricing

Website prices are indicative ranges reflecting hours of handwork. The final quote — fixed before you approve — depends on fabric, embellishment density and sizing.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India; courts at Anand, Gujarat have jurisdiction. For any dispute, we will always try to make it right first.
