The House

One woman. One needle. Every piece.

VM Design Studio is a hand-embroidery atelier in Anand, Gujarat — founded and run by a single artisan. What began as a frame, a hook needle and a love for traditional zari has grown into a couture practice with brides from India to the USA, UK and UAE.

One artisan, end to end

You commission one person, not a supply chain. Sketching, fabric, embroidery, finishing — same hands.

Hand zari & aari only

No machine embroidery. Every motif is couched, chained or beaded by needle, by hand.

Made to order, never stock

Nothing is mass-produced. Each piece is started only when commissioned — yours alone.

Honest craft, honest price

You pay for hours of skilled handwork — and we tell you exactly what those hours are.
