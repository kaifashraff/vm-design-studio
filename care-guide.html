Caring for Hand Embroidery

Last updated: 8 August 2026

← Back to home

Your piece is alive — treat it gently

Hand embroidery is delicate by nature. With simple care, a VM piece will outlive trends — and most wardrobes.

Cleaning

  • Dry-clean only. Never machine-wash or hand-wash embroidered pieces.
  • Choose a dry-cleaner experienced with zari and heavy embroidery; tell them it is hand-embroidered.
  • Spot-clean minor marks with a clean, dry cloth — blot, never rub.

Storage

  • Store flat, or hanging with padded shoulders on a wide wooden hanger.
  • Wrap in a breathable cotton/muslin garment bag — never plastic (trapped moisture dulls zari).
  • Keep away from direct sunlight and damp walls; silica gel in the cupboard helps.
  • Do not stack heavy garments on top of embroidery.

Wearing

  • Apply perfume, deodorant and hair products before dressing — alcohol tarnishes metallic threads.
  • Avoid sitting on heavily embroidered areas for long periods; zari can flatten.
  • Jewellery can snag threads — put pieces on after your outfit is settled.

Repairs

If a thread loosens, do not pull it. Tuck it gently to the back and message us on WhatsApp — we will guide you, or repair the piece if you send it back to the atelier.
