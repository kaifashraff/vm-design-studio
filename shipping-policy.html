Shipping Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

← Back to home

Worldwide, insured, tracked

Every VM Design Studio piece ships from our atelier in Anand, Gujarat, India via insured courier with a tracking number. You will receive tracking the moment your piece leaves us, and we stay available on WhatsApp until it arrives.

Delivery times (from dispatch)

  • India: 2–4 working days
  • UAE & Middle East: 3–5 working days
  • USA & Canada: 5–7 working days
  • UK & Europe: 6–8 working days
  • Australia & New Zealand: 7–10 working days
  • Rest of the world: 7–12 working days

Made-to-order timeline

Because pieces are hand-embroidered to order, please add craft time before dispatch: jackets 3–4 weeks, bridal lehengas 8–10 weeks, bespoke couture 6–8 weeks, adornments 2–3 weeks. Your exact date is confirmed before we begin — and for wedding deadlines we plan backwards from your date.

Duties & taxes

Prices are in INR and do not include import duties, customs or local taxes. These are payable at entry by the recipient and vary by country. We declare the accurate value of every shipment — under-declaring is never done. If you would like an estimate of duties before ordering, ask us on WhatsApp and we will help you calculate it.

Packaging

Each piece is wrapped in acid-free tissue, packed with silica gel, and shipped in a rigid, waterproof carton — the same care we would give it in our own cupboard.
