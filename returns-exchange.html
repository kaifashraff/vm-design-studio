Made to order, made right

Every VM piece is hand-embroidered and cut to your measurements — it does not exist in a warehouse. Because it is made only for you, orders are final sale. This is how we keep prices honest and quality uncompromised.

What we guarantee instead

  • Fitting: if a made-to-measure piece needs adjustment, we guide you through alterations at our cost (within India, or with a trusted local tailor internationally — we reimburse reasonable alteration costs).
  • Craftsmanship: if any embroidery flaw occurs within 30 days of delivery, we repair or re-work the piece at no charge.
  • Shipping: if your piece arrives damaged, we replace it — the shipment is insured for exactly this.
  • Before dispatch: you approve your piece via photos and video before it ships. If it is not what you agreed, we fix it before it leaves the atelier.

Cancellations

Orders may be cancelled within 48 hours of the advance payment, with a full refund. After craft begins, the advance is non-refundable — materials are cut and embroidery is underway.

Refunds

Refunds (where applicable) are processed to the original payment method within 7 working days of approval.
