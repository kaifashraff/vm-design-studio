Your information, our responsibility

VM Design Studio respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and why, in plain language.

What we collect

  • When you message us: your name, WhatsApp number, order details, measurements and delivery address — only what you share with us.
  • When you email us: your email address and the content of your message.

How we use it

  • To make, price, fit and deliver your order.
  • To communicate about your order and respond to enquiries.
  • With your consent, to share collection updates (you can opt out anytime).

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We never store payment card numbers — payments happen through your bank, UPI, Razorpay or PayPal, on their secure platforms.

Data retention & your rights

Order records are kept as long as needed for tax and warranty purposes. You may request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp.
