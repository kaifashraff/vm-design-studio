The House

A boutique with one pair of hands

VM Design Studio is not a factory with a front office. It is a single artisan's atelier — one woman in Anand, Gujarat, who sketches, selects, embroiders and finishes every piece herself.

No middlemen, no mass production, no compromise. When you commission a VM piece, you are commissioning the hours, eyes and judgement of one dedicated craftsperson — the same person who answers your WhatsApp.

The Craft

Not mass-produced. Hand-made.

Behind every VM piece is a frame, a needle and hours of patient handwork. This is the craft that has dressed India for centuries — and it still happens one stitch at a time.

Hand Zardozi

Gold and silver threads, couched by hand into motifs that have adorned Indian weddings for five centuries.

Hand Aari

A hooked needle works through stretched fabric, building chain-stitch embroidery that shimmers with movement.

Embellishment

Beads, sequins and stones placed by hand — detail you can feel, not just see.
