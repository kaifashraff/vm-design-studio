The Atelier

Not mass-produced. Hand-made.

Behind every VM piece is a frame, a needle and hours of patient handwork. This is the craft that has dressed India for centuries — and it still happens one stitch at a time.

Hand Aari

A hooked needle works through stretched fabric, building chain-stitch embroidery that shimmers with movement. The foundation of our signature pieces.

Hand Zari

Gold and silver threads, couched by hand into the motifs that have adorned Indian weddings for five centuries. Zari is not printed — it is placed, thread by thread.
